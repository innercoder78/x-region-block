# Third-party notices

## Country flag PNG files

The bundled country flag PNG pack in `src/assets/flags/` comes from
[Flagpedia's flag icon download page](https://flagpedia.net/download/icons). Flagpedia states that
the flag artwork is sourced through Wikimedia Commons and is in the public domain. The supplied
pack contains 20-pixel-wide PNG renderings preserving the flags' original aspect ratios.

X — Region Reveal & Block does not claim ownership of the flag artwork. The files are bundled
locally with the extension and are never downloaded from Flagpedia, Wikimedia Commons, or another
remote host while browsing X.
